            How to play

            A shield is hidden: work out whose arms these are. Type the name of a state, a province, a noble house or a person, and pick it from the list.

            No idea? Ask for a clue: a tincture (gules), a division (quarterly), an ordinary (cross), a charge (lion). The page says whether it's on the mystery shield, at no cost in guesses; only the arms you name count as guesses. Clues are counted, and break ties on the leaderboard.

            Three levels, one shield each: Squire (simple, well-known arms, eight guesses), Knight (six guesses), Lord (the hardest, five guesses, no category shown).

            Each answer shows its arms and compares them with the mystery shield, box by box:

            • ✓ identical
            • ≈ close: a tincture or a charge in common, or of the same family
            • ✗ different

            The mystery shield is revealed little by little: first just its outline, then its divisions, its ordinaries, and its charges on the third guess. The tinctures (the colours of heraldry) you find light up on it; the others stay grey.

            Really stuck? “Give up”, below the input, reveals the shield: the game then counts as a loss.

            New shields every day at midnight (Paris time), the same for everyone. Stuck? That's what the encyclopedia is for.

            The Armorial — the daily game and the encyclopedia of heraldry, in French for now. In French, this game is called L'Écu du jour. The mystery arms and the answers are drawn by the Atelier engine, from the blazons of the cards in Real arms and People; the actual image shown at the end comes from Wikimedia Commons, credited below the answer. English names and notes on the arms are translated from the French pages.

            By default nothing is sent anywhere: your guesses, scores and profile stay in this browser. If you create an online account (optional, with Google or an e-mail), your e-mail, username, arms, scores and library are kept by Firebase (Google); your e-mail is only used to write to you if you tick the news box, and you can delete everything from your profile.
