Practice

Read a shield, picture its arms, judge the rule of tincture, name the points of the shield: freshly drawn questions every time, with the answer explained, and a daily challenge that is the same for everyone.

Methodwhere the shields, the texts and the answers come from

Each shield is composed at random by the engine of the Atelier, and each blazon is the one the Atelier writes for it. A question is only asked if the blazon reader rereads the French text and finds exactly the same arms, so what you are asked to read is unambiguous and contains nothing invented. That check runs on the French blazon, which remains the reference; the English text you see is written from the same arms by a separate writer.

The wrong answers are neighbouring shields, which differ from the right one by a single feature: the tincture of the field, the number of charges, the ordinary, the direction. After each answer, the words that make the difference are underlined.

The vocabulary definitions come from the encyclopedia's glossary, hence the link to each term; the names of the points of the shield follow the chapter “The shield”, and the rule of tincture the chapter “Tinctures”.

Nothing is sent anywhere: your best streaks and the result of the daily challenge stay in this browser and nowhere else.
